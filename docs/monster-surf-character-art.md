# Monster Surf: seven redrawn surfing characters

Generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool using each original character as identity reference. No 3D model. Every new sheet contains six fully redrawn poses: straight, left carve, right carve, celebration, hurt and fall. The surfboard is included. Original generated PNGs and alpha are preserved.

Files: `public/monster-surf/characters/M0-surf.png` through `M6-surf.png`. Alpha crops and consistent scale anchors are recorded in `features/monster-surf/surfCharacterArt.ts`. The original portraits remain unmodified.

M4–M6 exact prompts/provenance: [monster-surf-characters-M4-M6.md](monster-surf-characters-M4-M6.md).

## M0 prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: complete 2D game surfing action SPRITE SHEET. Reference image identifies the original character only. REDRAW its entire body in genuine surfing actions and varying viewing angles; do NOT paste the standing reference on a surfboard.
Canvas landscape 1536x1024, THREE columns and TWO rows, SIX isolated poses, exact regular 512x512 cells. Every cell includes ONE complete monster and ONE surfboard, centered, generous 30px clear transparent gutters on all sides. Keep character and board pixel scale consistent across cells. All six figures fully inside their cells.
High 45-degree three-quarter OVERHEAD camera suitable for downward-scrolling surfing game. Perspective fixed across sheet; nose of surfboard points DOWN toward viewer, turns point diagonally lower-left/lower-right. Genuine athletic pose anatomy, lowered center of gravity, bent legs/feet gripping board, expressive arms counterbalancing. Original 2D hand-drawn soft colored-pencil/cartoon style, pastel fur with clean dark colored sketch outlines and simple cel-shaded forms. No 3D/plastic rendering. Surfboard is cream with single broad teal central stripe and charcoal rear grip, SAME design everyframe.
POSES IN EXACT READING ORDER:
Top-left: straight surf downward toward viewer, low balanced stance, front three-quarter view.
Top-center: carving LEFT, entire board angled 30 degrees toward LOWER LEFT, torso turned toward left, shoulders twist, weight onleft edge, leftward side/threequarter anatomy.
Top-right: carving RIGHT, board angled 30 degrees toward LOWER RIGHT, torso turned right and oppositely counterbalanced arms, rightward side/threequarter anatomy.
Bottom-left: successful celebratory ride, confident joyous expression, arms or fist raised, body leaning back slightly, board stilldownward.
Bottom-center: collision STAGGER, surprised/distressed face, one foot lifts, body tipping and arms flailing for balance, board tilts.
Bottom-right: WIPEOUT FALL, character tumbling sideways in midair next to its tilted surfboard, visibly separated from board, startled face.
No water or waves, no particles, no splash, no shadow on ground, no ocean, no labels/cellnumbers/titles/borders/checkerboard. Preserve the appropriate DAY text only on visible belly where naturally fits. Actual fully TRANSPARENT alpha background everywhere outside six sprites.
CHARACTER IDENTITY & DISTINCT SURFING PERSONALITY:
Sunday: soft lavender round wide sleepy furry blob, pale belly with SUN, tiny floppy feet, sleepy closed eyes. Its unique style is a relaxed very LOW belly/kneeling surfer, soft arms spread for balance; open eyes only when surprised. Preserve this rounded horizontal silhouette, not a tall humanoid.
